Questions

You Asked.
We Answer.

Do you only work with registered NGOs? +
No, we work with registered NGOs, Section 8 companies, social enterprises, CSR implementation agencies, development consultancies, and individual social entrepreneurs. If your work creates measurable positive change, we want to be part of telling that story.
We're a small organisation with a very limited budget. Is there flexibility? +
Yes. We have a mission-first pricing philosophy. If you're working in an underserved area, operating with government or grassroots funding, or are in your first three years, we offer a subsidised rate on request. We also have a pro-bono slot for one organisation per quarter ask us about it.
How quickly can you start on a project? +
Typically within 48–72 hours of signing the agreement and receiving your brief. For urgent work a funder presentation due tomorrow, an event coming up this week we have a fast-track option. WhatsApp us directly and we'll tell you what's possible.
Will we own all the content and designs you create? +
Yes, fully. Upon final payment, all intellectual property source files, brand assets, videos, website code, copy transfers completely to you. No ongoing licensing fees. No hidden restrictions. Everything we make for you is yours.
We've had bad experiences with agencies not understanding our work. How is this different? +
Fair. The difference is context: our team has worked inside the development sector not just for it. We understand the difference between outputs and outcomes, between an annual report for a donor and one for an auditor, between what looks good in a CSR pitch and what actually converts. We don't need explaining to. We start from fluency.
Can you handle both Hindi/Telugu and English content? +
Yes. We work fluently in English, Hindi, and Telugu for written and visual content. For other regional languages, we work with verified translation partners who understand the context of social development communication not just literal translation.
What does the onboarding process look like? +
Discovery call (30 min) → proposal with scope, timeline, and pricing (within 48 hrs) → agreement sign-off → kick-off meeting with your brief → first deliverables within 72 hrs. That's the whole process. We believe good work starts with a clear brief and fast momentum, not endless paperwork.
Do you work on one-off projects or only retainer? +
Both. Monthly retainers are where we can do our best work consistent presence, deep context, compounding results. But we also do excellent one-off projects: annual reports, brand redesigns, event videos, CSR proposal decks, website builds. Project pricing is scoped per brief.
